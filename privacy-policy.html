SpoilerShield Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · applies to the SpoilerShield browser extension, version 1.0.0 and later

Summary

SpoilerShield does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data or browsing data. Everything it does happens inside your browser.

What the extension processes

To hide content you don't want to see, SpoilerShield reads the text of pages on the sites it supports: YouTube, Reddit, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok and Google Search/News. It reads titles, captions, comments, image alt text and link addresses, and compares them with your keyword list. This happens entirely on your device. Page content is not saved and never leaves your browser.

What the extension stores

  • Your settings: on/off state, mode, per-site switches and keyword lists, stored with chrome.storage.local on your device. They are not synced to your Google account.
  • Counts of hidden items: numbers only, per site. No record is kept of what was hidden or which pages you visited.
  • The current tab's count, kept in chrome.storage.session, which the browser clears when it closes.

You can delete all of this at any time by removing the extension, or reset the counts from its settings page.

What the extension does not do

  • It makes no network requests. There are no servers, analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party services.
  • It does not use AI or cloud services, and it does not upload images or text for analysis.
  • It does not track your browsing history, and it does not run on any website other than the six listed above.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone, because it has none.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your settings and counts on your device.
  • Access to the six supported sites: needed to find and hide matching posts, videos and comments on those pages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. A change that involves collecting data would require your consent first.
